Details
8in1 Delights Chicken Bones L are made of tasty beefhide and delicious chicken breast and ensure intensive, long-lasting chewing fun for large and giant dogs with a weight exceeding 25kg. More than this: while keeping your dog busy, the bones enhance dental care. Due to the natural abrasive action of chewing, tartar is removed from your dog's teeth and in that way the bones support your dog's dental health. The low in fat 8in1 Delights Chicken Bones L perfectly serve as a mouth-watering chew to reward your dog. They are rich in proteins and free from artificial colours and flavour enhancers. Furthermore, neither sugar nor GMO's or gluten are added to the oven baked chews.
Nutritional Facts
Dosage
Feed 1 treat over a course of two days. Do not exceed 1 treat in a two day period. Treats should not exceed 10% of your dog’s daily caloric intake.
Application
Select a chew slightly larger than your pet’s mouth. Balance food intake and exercise with this reward snack. Always provide fresh water. Supervise your dog while chewing.
