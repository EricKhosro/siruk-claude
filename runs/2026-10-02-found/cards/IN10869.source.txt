8in1 Multivitamin Tablets are perfect as a daily supplement for senior dogs. They are specially formulated to meet the nutritional needs of older dogs and to help prevent problems associated with diet related deficiencies.
8in1 Multivitamin Tablets provide your dog with a balanced mix of 12 vitamins, including vitamins A, C and E, which act as natural antioxidants, as well as vitamin D and essential minerals for example magnesium, calcium and zinc to keep your dog healthy and active. As senior dogs often experience decreased nutrient absorption, our 8in1 Multivitamin tablets contain higher levels of vitamins and minerals than supplements for adult dogs. Combined with your dog's regular food, 8in1 Multivitamin tablets make sure your senior dog gets the nutrition he needs. Developed with veterinarians, our food supplements are free from artificial colours or preservatives. 8in1 Multivitamin Tablets for senior dogs are made in Germany.
