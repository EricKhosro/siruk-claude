 Description
The Beaphar Soft Cat Flea Collar contains the natural plant extracts Margosa and Lavandin Oil to repel fleas for up to 8 weeks. The collar requires 5-7 days to reach optimal effect. 
This comfortable fabric collar has been fitted with quick release safety buckle, which is designed to break apart if your cat gets caught while out and about, offering greater peace of mind for you. The collar can also be adjusted to fit any cat.
The Beaphar Soft Reflective Cat Flea Collar has a reflective yellow finish to help your cat to be seen more easily at night, helping to keep them safe. It also comes complete with an optional silver bell.
Suitable for cats and kittens from 12 weeks of age.
95% of a flea problem is in the home in the form of flea eggs and other developing flea life stages. For best results it is recommended to use a household insecticide to treat your home, such as Beaphar FLEAtec Household Flea Spray.
 Instructions
ALWAYS FULLY READ THE PRODUCT PACKAGING BEFORE USE.
Do not remove the collar from the blister until you are ready to use the product.
1. Fasten the collar around your cat’s neck (coloured side facing outwards) leaving enough room to insert two fingers between the collar and neck
2. Cut off any collar sticking out beyond the buckle. Wrap this cut-off in a bag and throw it in your dustbin.
3. Remember to periodically check and loosen the collar if fitted to a growing cat.
For continuous protection, fit a new Beaphar Soft Flea Collar every eight weeks.

 Storage and safety
 Store below 25°C. Store in the original packaging until ready to use. Keep away from food and drink, including animal feeding stuffs. Dispose of any excess collar length and the used collar wrapped in a bag in the household rubbish. Empty packaging can also be disposed of in the household rubbish. Dispose of contents/container in accordance with local regulations. This material and its container must be disposed of in a safe way. Keep out of reach of children. Remove the collar at the first sign of intolerance. If medical advice is needed, have product container or label at hand. Do not contaminate foodstuffs, eating utensils or food contact surfaces. Wash hands and exposed skin before meals and after use.
