Monge Monoprotein Dog Solo Chicken is a complete wet food for adult dogs, developed to provide a balanced diet for everyday feeding. The recipe is based on a single source of animal protein – chicken, which provides high-quality, easily digestible protein with high biological value.

Composition: Chicken 65% (equivalent to 100% of the meat used), minerals. Analytical constituents: Crude protein 7.5%, crude fibre 0.3%, crude fat 6%, crude ash 2%, moisture 80%.
