Monge Monoprotein Dog Fruit Turkey with Citrus Fruits is a complete wet food for adult dogs, combining a high-quality source of animal protein with fruit and tapioca. The recipe is based on a single source of animal protein – turkey, which provides high-quality, easily digestible protein with high biological value. The addition of citrus fruits enriches the recipe with plant-based ingredients and gives it a distinctive character.

Composition: Turkey 65% (equivalent to 100% of the meat used), tapioca 5%, citrus fruits 4%, minerals. Analytical constituents: Crude protein 8%, crude fibre 0.5%, crude fat 6.5%, crude ash 1.5%, moisture 80%.
