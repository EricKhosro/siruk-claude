Monge Natural Superpremium
Monge Natural Superpremium Sterilised Rich in Chicken
400 g - 1,5 kg - 10 kg
The Monge Natural Superpremium line ensures the wellness of the animal thanks to the correct balance of essential nutritional elements, a result of Made in Italy research. Monge Natural Superpremium Sterilised Rich in Chicken is a complete food for adult cats. Formulated with chicken as its main ingredient and with salmon oil, a rich source of omega-3 fatty acids, for soft and shiny fur. The formulation is enriched with XOS (Xylo-oligosaccharides) and MOS (mannan-oligosaccharides), prebiotics that promote intestinal wellness. The formulation has a low fat* content to help the maintenance of ideal body weight and it is high in fibre* to limit the energetic density of the product. No added dyes and artificial preservatives.
*Compared to standard product (Monge Natural Superpremium Adult Cat Rich in Chicken).

Composition
Chicken (26% dehydrated meat, 10% fresh), wheat, corn gluten, corn, hydrolysed animal protein (liver), animal fat (chicken oil purified at 99.5%), pea fibre (3%), dried egg, fish (dried salmon), fish oil 2% (salmon oil purified at 99.5%), minerals, yeast products (mannan-oligo-saccharides MOS 1%), Xylo-oligosaccharide (XOS 0.3%), products from the processing of herbs (rose hip 0.1%), Yucca schidigera (0.1%).
Analytical components
Crude protein: 35%, Crude fibre: 5%, Crude fats: 10%, Crude ash: 6.0%, Calcium: 1.2%, Phosphorus: 1%, Magnesium: 0.08%, Sodium: 0.2%, n-3 fatty acids: 0.4%, n-6 fatty acids: 3.6%. Metabolisable Energy (Atwater calculation): 3,840 kcal/kg.
Additives
NUTRITIONAL ADDITIVES/kg: Vitamin A (retinyl acetate) 26,000 IU, Vitamin D3 1,830 IU, Vitamin E (all-rac-alpha-tocopheryl-acetate 3a700i) 130 mg, Selenium (Sodium selenite 0.2 mg) 0.09 mg, Manganese (manganous sulphate monohydrate 32 mg) 10.4 mg, Zinc (Zinc oxide 150 mg): 120.4 mg, Copper (Copper (II) sulphate pentahydrate 13 mg) 3.3 mg, Iron (Iron (II) sulphate monohydrate 110 mg) 39.2 mg, Iodine (Calcium iodate anhydrous 1.8 mg) 1.17 mg; L-Carnitine 700 mg; technically pure DL-Methionine: 2,500 mg, taurine 2,400 mg. TECHNOLOGICAL ADDITIVES: Antioxidants: Tocopherol extracts from vegetable oils.
Instructions for use
Daily feeding intakes may be adapted according to the cat’s size, age and living conditions. The recommended daily intakes indicated in the table may be split into two meals. Fresh and clean water should be available at all times. When this product replaces another type of feed and/or diet, it is recommended to introduce it gradually over a period of at least 1 week. Cat food, not suitable for human consumption.
