Monge BWild Grain Free Formula Sterilised Cat Tuna with Peas
1,5 kg
Monge Natural Superpremium BWild Grain Free Formula Sterilised Cat Tuna with peas is a complete and balanced pet food for adult sterilised cats. Formulated for the wellbeing of your sterilised cat thanks to the L-Carnitine and DL-Methionine content to help the ideal body weight control. The formulation is characterized by a selection of alternative sources of carbohydrates not derivate from cereals (peas) and high quality ingredients (as Tuna). The recipe is enriched by a synergic complex of prebiotics in mannan-oligosaccharides (MOS) and xylo-oligosaccharide (XOS), which keep the intestinal wellness. The formulation helps to maintain the shiny coat through the inclusion of omega-3 fatty acids. It contains taurine to support the cardiac functions. It is formulated with the inclusion of spiruline and yucca schidigera, for intestinal aroma control. No added dyes and artificial preservatives.

Composition
tuna (dried 38%, fresh 15%), potatoes (15%), animal fat (10%) (chicken oil pu- rified 99.5%), potato protein, dried beet pulp, peas (5%), pea fibre, hydrolysed animal proteins, dried fish salmon (4%), minerals, fish oil (salmon oil purified at 99.5%), mannan-oligosacchari- des (MOS 1%), spiruline (0.6%), xylo-oligosaccharide (XOS 0.3%), yucca schidigera (0.1%), milk protein powder.
Analytical components
Crude Protein 33%, Crude Fibre 6%, Crude fat 11%, Crude Ash 7.5%, Calcium 1.4%, Phosphorus 1.0%, Magnesium 0.08%, Sodium 0.6%, n-3 fatty acids: 0.5%, n-6 fatty acids: 2.5%.
Additives
NUTRITIONAL ADDITIVES/kg: Vitamin A 34,300 IU, Vitamin D3 2,000 IU, Vitamin E (all-rac-alpha-tocopheryl acetate) 490 mg, selenium (sodium selenite 0.44 mg): 0.3 mg, manganese (manganous sulphate monohydrate 95 mg): 32 mg, zinc (zinc oxide 196 mg): 150 mg, copper (copper (II) sulphate pentahydrate 48 mg): 12 mg, iron (iron (II) sulphate monohydrate 340 mg): 108 mg, iodine (calcium iodate anhydrous 2.6 mg): 1.7 mg, DL-methionine technically pure 1,000 mg, L-Carnitine 1,000 mg, Taurine 1,200 mg. TECHNOLOGICAL ADDITIVES: Tocopherol extracts from vegetable oils.
Instructions for use
Recommended daily feeding intakes (see table) may be split into 2 daily meals. Indi- vidual nutritional requirements may vary due to size, age and living conditions of the animal. Fresh and clean water should be available at all times. When this product replaces another type of feed and/or diet it is recommended to introduce it gradually over a period of at least 1 week. Cat food only, not suitable for human consumption.
